The Spirit of Cricket
Under the sun, the players stand,
A bat, a ball, a cheering stand.
The bowler runs, the batter waits,
A single delivery decides their fates.

The ball flies high across the sky,
The fielders leap, the crowds all cry.
A boundary roars, six runs take flight,
The stadium glows with pure delight.

Through every wicket, every run,
The battle rages till it's done.
With courage, teamwork, and dreams so bright,
Each player fights with all their might.

And when the final ball is bowled,
A thousand stories will be told.
For cricket is more than a game we play,
It's passion, pride, and hope in every way.
